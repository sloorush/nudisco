# Privacy Policy — nudisco by enchante

**Effective date:** 18 June 2026

nudisco ("the app") is a local-network silent-disco listener published by
enchante ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do
with your information. The short version: **we don't collect, store, sell, or
share any personal data.**

## What we collect
**Nothing.** nudisco has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising,
no third-party trackers, and no servers that we operate. We do not collect or
receive any personal information from your device.

## How the app works (and why it needs certain permissions)
nudisco connects directly to a DJ's computer **on the same Wi-Fi network** and
plays the audio it broadcasts. Audio is streamed peer-to-peer over your local
network; it is **not recorded** and **does not pass through, or get stored on,
any server operated by us or anyone else.**

The app asks for these device permissions, used **only on your device**:

- **Local Network** — to find and connect to the DJ's computer on your Wi-Fi.
  No data is sent off your local network.
- **Microphone** — the underlying audio engine (WebRTC) initializes the device's
  audio hardware, which on iOS requires this permission. **nudisco never records,
  uses, stores, or transmits microphone audio.** The app sends no audio from your
  device at all (it only receives).
- **Camera** — used only to scan the join QR code shown on the DJ's screen. The
  camera image is processed on-device and is never stored or transmitted.

A small preference (your chosen audio buffer setting) is stored locally on your
device using the system's standard preferences storage. It never leaves your
device.

## Data sharing
We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The app
contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Children
nudisco is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information
from anyone.

## Your choices
You can revoke the Local Network, Microphone, or Camera permissions at any time in
**Settings ▸ nudisco**. (Note: the audio engine needs microphone access to start;
denying it may prevent playback on some iOS versions.)

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date.
